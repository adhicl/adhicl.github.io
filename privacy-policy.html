Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YourFavoriteGameStudio ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with our mobile games (including Dungeon Match Quest, Neko Match Blast, Number Tower, and Jajanan: Snack Sort Puzzle) and this website (together, the "Services").

You don't need an account to play most of our games, and we don't collect or sell personal data ourselves. Jajanan offers optional Google Sign-In for cloud save and leaderboards — see "Google Sign-In & Play Games Services" below. Almost everything else on this page comes from the third-party advertising and analytics providers we use to keep our games free.

Information collected by third-party services

Our games use ads and analytics to stay free to play. We use the following third-party services, which may collect information automatically on their own behalf, under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — shows ads in our games and may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. Google's ad policy
  • Google Analytics / Firebase Analytics — helps us understand how players use our games (e.g. sessions, crashes, feature usage) using device and usage data. Google's privacy policy
  • Google Play Services — the platform our Android games run on, which has its own data practices. Google's privacy policy

You can see a full, current list of ad-serving partners authorized to sell our ad inventory in our app-ads.txt file.

Google Sign-In & Play Games Services (Jajanan)

Jajanan: Snack Sort Puzzle uses your Google Account to securely authenticate your profile, back up your game progress to Google Cloud, and display your achievements on Google Play Games leaderboards. We do not access or store your personal contacts, emails, or private data. Google's privacy policy

Signing in with Google is optional — Jajanan can be played without an account, but connecting one syncs your progress across devices and unlocks leaderboards and achievements. You can disconnect Jajanan's access to your Google Account at any time from your Google Account permissions settings.

What we don't do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not collect payment details, contact lists, photos, or other personal data beyond what the ad, analytics, and Google Sign-In services above collect automatically. We don't operate our own servers that store player data — game progress synced via Google Sign-In is stored in Google's cloud infrastructure, governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising, or reset/limit your advertising ID, in your device's settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking).
  • You can uninstall a game at any time to stop all data collection associated with that app.
  • App store privacy labels (Google Play "Data safety" / Apple "App Privacy") list what each specific game collects.

Children's privacy

Our games are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Data security

We rely on reputable third-party providers (Google) who maintain their own security standards for the data they process. We don't operate our own servers that store player data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
